The Imperfect Child Who Cracked a Smile

Section I: The Noise

Serenity's Institution

Life's a constant rollercoaster of shame,
Leaving my heart, body, and soul chatting with pain.
These dark perceptions keep my mind in question: Is my life a lesson?
I pray for my brother's sake, that my mind won't serve as their reflection.
Hope you let them live on the surface and not past it,
Fore this life is truly drastic.
From buddies in elementary, to full rides to penitentiaries,
Vape smoke and pills confiscating your classroom memories.
Now you're making enemies, fore your thinking is of the contrast,
Due to the trends and norms you look past.
Now your scene is like errors within a rough draft,
Leaving you to ponder: Is this the life I must have?
That movie is what I don't want young Max or Mason to see,
Fore there's no scripts or censorship to shield the content you see.
All I want is a happy ending, I already missed the beginning.
I asked Serenity, "Can your definition cease my intermission from ticking?"
Or maybe I need an institution for my mentality-
I love myself too much to be my own fatality.

I Talked to God Yesterday

I talked to God yesterday.
He told me to walk within His words and don't let the Devil have a say.
My Bible sits on the floor as my demons knock on my door.
I can't get myself to pray to keep the problems at shore,
So I exhale tree smoke as my temporary cure.
I've been lying to my mom, knowing my life's not pure.
Got intoxicated last night because I felt too insecure.
Momma told me to love who you are, but the child in front of her words
Traveled pretty far.
I asked God to take the wheel on my journey and still I crashed the car.
My heart was never in it while I wished on a star.
Mental scars to serve as a reminder of my faults.
I should be seeking therapy instead of questioning my thoughts.
I give the world my all by default, while the crowd goes mute when I talk.

Mr. Loner

I stay alone
Within a world where love is a loan.
If my time is money, then the hours
I invest in you should be time spent on my own.
I question my choices
While blocking the noises.
Can't sleep when the voices keep me voiceless.
I'm conflicted in my current restriction.
I want to see you happy,
But not in this current description.
Guess I need to change my perception with these new prescriptions.

The Snake and Me

Scenes played out,
Feelings bled out,
Words left out.
Through love, the pain came and gave a shout.
This is why I'm always left in doubt.
When I think a rose will sprout,
The snake peeks out,
Leaving my mouth mute to speak out.
Vulnerability serves as an enemy.
Opening my book to you appears as a waste of energy,
But I never met a snake with such synergy.
We laugh,
We kid.
Part of me ponders on what it will look like if we have a kid.
But who am I to speak on what we did?
Maybe if I kept the fence above the grass,
Me and you could have been friends in the end.

Section III: The Release

Why

I can't put my trials into words,
Nor can I explain why my problems occur.
When my overthinking spurs,
I often reminisce on past events before I matured.
Early sexual encounters left me desensitized,
As I wonder, "What types of lessons did those interactions provide?"
I look up to the ceiling to find the truth within the answer,
But my pain still stays uncured like cancer.
I love my mother, but still I'd rather explain my troubles to a pole dancer.
I want to indulge in love and tell my wife the vices I have before she gives me a hug,
But I'm not where I want to be in life yet.
I hope the time that I spent rebuilding won't leave me in debt.

Brina

I tried to show you love and you covered your eyes.
I wanted to make you mine.
I thought it was possible since we put in the time.
Seen you in New York for the first time in 2 years.
Our hug and embrace felt sincere,
But the communication wasn't clear.
With us at our separate ways across oceans and towns,
I pray you can experience the same emotions I felt with you
With the next man you decide to let in now.
